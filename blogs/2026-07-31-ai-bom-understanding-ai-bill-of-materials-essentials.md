Key Takeaways An AI-BOM, or AI bill of materials, records the models, datasets, prompts, embeddings, and external AI services an AI system depends on. As organizations deploy more AI, maintaining that inventory has become as important as maintaining an SBOM for traditional software. The term collides with a much older one.

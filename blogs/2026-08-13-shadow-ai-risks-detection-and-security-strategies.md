Key Takeaways Shadow AI is any use of artificial intelligence inside an organization that no one approved, registered, or monitored. It covers consumer chat tools, AI features switched on inside approved software, desktop agents installed on laptops, and model endpoints running in unregistered cloud accounts. That definition is settled ground.

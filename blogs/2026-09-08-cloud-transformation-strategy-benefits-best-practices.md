Key Takeaways Cloud transformation is the change an organization makes to how it builds, runs, and funds software once its workloads live in cloud computing environments. It covers the move itself and what the move was meant to enable. Deployment practices change, capacity decisions change, and so does ownership of what breaks at 2 am.
